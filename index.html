PROFILE

About Me

I am a Professor in the Department of Pharmaceutics at MM College of Pharmacy, Maharishi Markandeshwar (Deemed to be University), Mullana, Ambala, Haryana.

My academic and research interests include pharmaceutical formulation development, nanocarriers, drug delivery, biopharmaceutics, characterization and optimization of advanced pharmaceutical systems.
